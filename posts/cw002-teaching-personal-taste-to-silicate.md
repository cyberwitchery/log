## why?

this one is about generative art, which is even further from network fabrics
than the trolley was. the answer is the same as last time: the lab publishes
on what it finds interesting.

[i’ve recently hit 10 years of generative art
practice](https://blog.veitheller.de/Generative_art_over_the_years.html), and
i’ve been building my little studio since then. this note talks about what
happened when a procedural drawing system was given a memory.

## the paper

the machine watches the choices made inside the drawing system (palette,
material, density, placement, gesture) and remembers which combinations were
met with *love*, *meh*, or *hate*. the claim: with 2,196 of those quick
judgments over a vocabulary of 222 authored decisions, it recovers about 22%
of the variation in the judgments that follow, enough to change what appears
next, not enough to settle the matter.

the more interesting half of the note is about where that vocabulary breaks.
the compositions it cannot explain mark the edge of what taste will let
itself be turned into features, and many of the best results live in the
*hate* bucket.
